MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT — ACME GLOBAL, INC. <> SUMMITLINE DATA CORP.
=======================================================================
Effective Date: 2026-03-01  ·  MSA ID: ACME-MSA-2026-0117
Counter-parties:
   - ACME Global, Inc. ("Customer")    Tax ID: 83-1100442
   - Summitline Data Corp. ("Vendor")  Registered: Delaware, USA

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
   1.1 Vendor will provide a managed analytics ingestion platform (the
       "Service") permitting Customer to stream up to 5 billion events per
       day into Vendor's hosted environment for transformation, retention,
       and downstream egress to Customer-owned data warehouses.
   1.2 Specific services, service levels, and pricing are detailed in
       Statements of Work executed under this MSA. SOW-001 is attached and
       integrated by reference.

2. TERM AND TERMINATION
   2.1 Initial term: 36 months from the Effective Date. Auto-renewal for
       successive 12-month terms unless either party gives written notice of
       non-renewal at least 90 days prior to the then-current expiration.
   2.2 Termination for cause: either party may terminate on 30 days' written
       notice if the other party materially breaches and fails to cure within
       the notice period.
   2.3 Termination for convenience: Customer may terminate any SOW on 60 days'
       written notice. Vendor may not terminate for convenience.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
   3.1 Annual subscription: USD 1,400,000 net 30, invoiced quarterly in
       advance, subject to the volume discount schedule in Exhibit B.
   3.2 Overages billed monthly in arrears at $0.40 per 1M events above the
       contracted 5B/day cap. Overage line items must be itemised by source
       system on the invoice.
   3.3 Annual price escalation: limited to the lesser of 3% or the prior
       12-month US CPI-U. Escalations require 90-day advance notice.

4. DATA PROTECTION
   4.1 Vendor processes Customer Data solely to provide the Service. Vendor
       shall not use Customer Data to train, fine-tune, or otherwise improve
       any general-purpose models that Vendor offers to other customers.
   4.2 Vendor maintains SOC 2 Type II and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification
       throughout the term and shall provide annual evidence of continued
       certification within 30 days of issuance.
   4.3 Sub-processors: Vendor's current sub-processor list is attached as
       Exhibit C. New sub-processors require 30 days' advance notice and
       Customer's right to object on reasonable grounds.
   4.4 Personal data: where Customer Data includes EU personal data, the
       DPA dated 2026-02-14 governs. Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2)
       apply to cross-border transfers to the United States.

5. AVAILABILITY AND SERVICE LEVELS
   5.1 Vendor commits to 99.9% monthly availability of the ingestion control
       plane. Credits apply per SOW-001 Section 4.
   5.2 Maintenance windows: pre-announced no fewer than 5 business days in
       advance and counted outside the availability metric.

6. LIABILITY
   6.1 Aggregate liability cap: 2x the trailing 12 months of fees paid under
       the affected SOW, except for (i) breach of confidentiality, (ii) breach
       of data protection obligations, (iii) IP indemnification, and (iv)
       gross negligence or willful misconduct — for which liability is capped
       at 3x trailing 12 months of fees.
   6.2 Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or
       punitive damages, except where excluded by Section 6.1.

7. ASSIGNMENT
   7.1 Neither party may assign this MSA without the other's prior written
       consent, except to an affiliate or in connection with a merger, sale
       of substantially all assets, or change of control. The non-assigning
       party retains a termination right exercisable within 60 days of
       notice of any such assignment to a Customer competitor.
