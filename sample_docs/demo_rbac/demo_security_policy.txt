ACME GLOBAL SECURITY POLICY v3.2
================================
Owner: Compliance / Security  ·  Effective: 2026-01-01  ·  Review cycle: 12 months
Approver: VP, Compliance (Hadley Park)  ·  Document ID: ACME-SEC-001

1. SCOPE
   Applies to every ACME employee, contractor, and third-party vendor with logical
   or physical access to ACME systems, data, or premises. Subsidiaries inherit
   this policy unless an exemption is recorded in the ACME Policy Exemption
   Register (PER-2026).

2. CONTROL FRAMEWORK ALIGNMENT
   This policy is mapped to:
     - NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 (control families AC, AU, CM, IA, SC, SI)
     - ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A (clauses 5, 6, 8, 12, 13)
     - SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria CC6, CC7, CC8
     - PCI DSS v4.0 sections 3, 7, 8, 10 where cardholder data is in scope

3. DATA CLASSIFICATION
   ACME data is classified into four tiers, with handling rules below:
     PUBLIC       — marketing site, press kits. No restriction.
     INTERNAL     — internal wikis, runbooks, source code without secrets.
                    Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+); at-rest encryption optional.
     CONFIDENTIAL — customer PII, finance, contracts. AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3
                    in transit, access logged, RBAC enforced at storage layer.
     RESTRICTED   — payment card data, secret keys, M&A materials.
                    HSM-backed encryption, named-list access only, MFA mandatory,
                    audit log integrity gate verified daily.

4. ACCESS CONTROL
   4.1 All production access requires single sign-on with phishing-resistant MFA
       (FIDO2 hardware key or platform authenticator). SMS OTP is prohibited.
   4.2 Standing privileged access is forbidden. Just-in-time elevation via the
       ACME PAM ticket workflow is the only sanctioned path.
   4.3 Service accounts must rotate credentials at least every 90 days for
       INTERNAL data and every 30 days for CONFIDENTIAL or RESTRICTED data.
   4.4 RBAC enforcement is implemented at the persistence layer (database row
       level, object store payload filter) — never relying solely on the
       application tier.

5. INCIDENT RESPONSE
   5.1 Severity 1 (active intrusion, customer data exfiltration in progress):
       Page primary IR on-call within 5 minutes; activate the war room; CFO and
       General Counsel notified within 30 minutes.
   5.2 Severity 2 (potential breach, suspicious anomaly): Acknowledge within
       30 minutes, triage within 4 hours, write-up within 24 hours.
   5.3 Customer notification: required within 72 hours when CONFIDENTIAL or
       RESTRICTED data is involved (GDPR Article 33 alignment).

6. AUDIT AND EVIDENCE
   6.1 All authentication, authorization, and data access events are logged
       to an append-only audit store with SHA-256 hash chaining.
   6.2 Integrity of the audit chain is verified nightly by an automated job.
       A break triggers a Severity 1 incident automatically.
   6.3 Retention: 7 years for CONFIDENTIAL/RESTRICTED events, 1 year for
       INTERNAL, 90 days for PUBLIC.

7. EXCEPTIONS
   Any deviation must be recorded in PER-2026 with a business justification,
   compensating controls, and an expiry date no later than 12 months from
   approval. Renewals require fresh sign-off.
