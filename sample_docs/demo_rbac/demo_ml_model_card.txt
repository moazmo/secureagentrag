ACME ML MODEL CARD — fraud-detector-v7
=======================================
Owner: Risk Engineering  ·  Status: Production (canary 5%, full rollout 2026-03-01)
Reviewers: Compliance (Tara Wexler), Privacy (Jin Park), Engineering Lead (Mo Saleh)

1. MODEL OVERVIEW
   - Task: binary classification — is the in-flight payment authorization
     attempt fraudulent (1) or legitimate (0)?
   - Algorithm: gradient-boosted decision trees (LightGBM) wrapped in an
     Isotonic calibrator. 832 features; 2,400 boosting rounds; max depth 7.
   - Training corpus: 18 months of card-not-present transactions across 4
     issuing regions, balanced 4:1 legitimate:fraud after under-sampling.
     Class weights: 1.0 / 3.7. PII (PAN, CVV, cardholder name, email) is
     tokenised before feature extraction. No raw PII leaves the secure
     enclave used for training.

2. INTENDED USE
   - In-line scoring of authorization attempts above $50 with risk score >= 0.4.
   - The model's output is one input among five used by the deterministic rule
     engine. The model is never a sole denial signal.
   - Out-of-scope: card-present transactions (different model, fraud-cp-v3);
     ACH transfers (different model, ach-detect-v2).

3. PERFORMANCE
   Holdout AUROC: 0.971  ·  PR-AUC: 0.873  ·  Recall@1%FPR: 0.642
   Demographic parity gap (region NA vs EMEA): 1.4% absolute (within the 3%
   compliance tolerance set in policy ACME-RISK-014).
   Equalised odds gap (region NA vs EMEA): 1.9% absolute.
   Latency p99 in production: 8.2 ms (one feature-store fetch + tree inference).

4. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
   - Trained on data through 2025-09-30; expect drift on novel fraud rings
     emerging after 2026-Q1. The monitor `fraud_v7_drift_psi` alerts when
     PSI > 0.2 on any of the top-20 most important features.
   - Holiday weeks (US Thanksgiving, EU summer holidays) show ~7% higher
     false-positive rate. The rule engine compensates by raising the
     model's denial threshold by 0.05 during these windows.
   - The model does not see CVV mismatch outcomes directly — the deterministic
     rule engine has higher signal there.

5. COMPLIANCE NOTES
   - GDPR Article 22 (automated individual decision-making): customers can
     request a human review of any decision where the model contributed to a
     denial. The customer-care intake form `cs-review-001` triggers this.
   - SR 11-7 model risk governance: model is rated "Tier 1" (material impact);
     subject to annual independent validation by the Model Risk team.
   - Fairness audit: completed 2026-02-20 by an external party (DataAudit Inc.,
     report DA-2026-019). Findings: no statistically significant adverse
     impact on any protected class at the alpha=0.05 level.

6. RETIRE / REPLACE
   - Retire condition: AUROC drops below 0.94 for 4 consecutive weeks, OR
     a successor model passes the v7 shadow gate at ≥1pp lift on PR-AUC.
   - Successor in training: fraud-detector-v8 (transformer-on-sequence,
     incorporates merchant-network embeddings). Shadow scoring since
     2026-02-01. Promote decision target: 2026-Q3.
