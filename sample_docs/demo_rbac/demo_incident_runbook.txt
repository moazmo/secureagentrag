ACME PRODUCTION INCIDENT RUNBOOK — checkout-service outage playbook
====================================================================
Owner: Platform SRE  ·  Last drilled: 2026-02-14  ·  Page audience: engineering on-call

1. SYMPTOM TRIAGE
   Common first signals (Datadog dashboards `checkout-svc-overview` and
   `checkout-svc-saturation`):
     - p99 latency on `POST /v2/checkout/commit` > 1500 ms for 3 consecutive minutes
     - 5xx rate on the same endpoint > 1.0% for any 1-minute bucket
     - Kafka consumer lag on `orders.committed` topic > 50k messages
     - Redis `INFO commandstats` shows `cmd_eval_lua` > 60% of total ops
       (we use Lua for the inventory reservation script; hot loop = stuck script)

2. IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (first 5 minutes)
   2.1 Page secondary on-call if not yet paged. Open the war-room Slack channel
       `#war-room-checkout` and post the alert link.
   2.2 Open the Datadog dashboard `checkout-svc-overview`. Screenshot the
       primary panels and paste into the war room.
   2.3 If the latency spike correlates with a deploy < 60 min old, prepare a
       rollback. Use `kubectl -n checkout rollout undo deploy/checkout-svc`.
       Do NOT run rollback until you have approval from incident commander.

3. ROLLBACK PROCEDURE
   3.1 Confirm previous-revision sha:  kubectl -n checkout rollout history
       deploy/checkout-svc.
   3.2 Take a heap dump of one pod before rollback if memory is suspicious:
       kubectl -n checkout exec <pod> -- jcmd 1 GC.heap_dump /tmp/dump.hprof
       kubectl -n checkout cp <pod>:/tmp/dump.hprof ./incident-<ID>/dump.hprof
   3.3 Execute: kubectl -n checkout rollout undo deploy/checkout-svc.
   3.4 Watch the deployment: kubectl -n checkout rollout status deploy/checkout-svc.
       Success criterion: all pods Ready within 4 minutes.
   3.5 Verify SLI recovery: latency back under 300 ms p99 within 5 minutes of
       rollout completion. If not, escalate to Severity 1.

4. ALTERNATIVE: DEPENDENCY ISOLATION
   When the root cause is downstream and rollback won't fix it:
   4.1 Trip the Hystrix breaker on the affected dependency by setting the
       feature flag `checkout_svc.disable_<dep>=true` in LaunchDarkly.
   4.2 Confirm the request stream now degrades gracefully (orders persist, the
       dependency is skipped, customer sees a "we'll confirm by email" page).
   4.3 Open a tracking ticket against the downstream team. Tag with `incident-<ID>`.

5. POST-INCIDENT
   5.1 Schedule a blameless postmortem within 72 hours of resolution.
   5.2 Required attendees: incident commander, primary on-call, downstream
       teams whose service was implicated. CEO is read-only on the doc.
   5.3 Action items go into the Engineering Effectiveness Board with owner
       and date. The board reviews progress every other Tuesday.

6. KNOWN PITFALLS
   - `kubectl rollout undo` skips PodDisruptionBudget — do not chain it with
     a node drain or you will scale below the PDB minimum and the cluster
     autoscaler will spin up cold nodes (~3 min boot).
   - The Lua reservation script in Redis MUST NOT call HGETALL on a hash with
     > 50k fields. We've seen 12-second blocking calls take the whole shard
     out. The new helper `inventory.cap_reserve_v3.lua` is the only blessed
     path; the v2 helper is still in the codebase but marked deprecated.
   - Do NOT scale the consumer group up to absorb lag during a Sev1. The
     downstream warehouse worker is the bottleneck — adding consumers only
     starves it of partitions.
